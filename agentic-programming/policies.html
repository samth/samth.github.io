4 Policies🔗

4.1 Attendance🔗

The course depends on in-person discussion, review, and studio work. Attendance expectations will be described in the full syllabus.

The first two scheduled class meetings, Tuesday, August 25 and Thursday, August 27, 2026, are cancelled.

4.2 AI Tools🔗

AI coding tools are the central subject of the course. Students should expect to use them, study their behavior, and critique their output.

Using an AI tool does not remove responsibility for submitted work. Students must be able to explain the code and design decisions they submit. When an assignment asks for process evidence, students must preserve the relevant prompts, transcripts, diffs, tests, and review notes.

4.3 Writing🔗

Writing submitted for this course must be written by the students named on the submission. Do not use LLMs or other AI tools to draft, rewrite, polish, or summarize course writing unless an assignment explicitly says that such use is part of the task. This includes paper lab notebook entries, reflections, design rationales, code reviews, README text, and project reports.

4.4 Academic Integrity🔗

The course will follow Indiana University’s academic misconduct policy. Because this course explicitly studies AI-assisted programming, the detailed collaboration and tool-use rules will be assignment specific and will be posted with each assignment.

4.5 Privacy and Data🔗

Use IU’s ChatGPT Edu workspace through your @iu.edu account for ordinary course Codex and ChatGPT work. IU describes ChatGPT Edu as intended for responsible use with university data and says information entered in IU’s ChatGPT Edu workspace is not used to train AI models. See Codex Access for setup instructions.

IU’s ChatGPT Edu guidance allows some use of IU data classified as Restricted, with exceptions, but does not allow Critical data. For this course, do not paste private, sensitive, confidential, legally restricted, credential, grade, Critical, research-restricted, or non-public third-party data into AI tools unless an assignment explicitly tells you to do so and explains the required handling. Public AI services outside IU’s approved workspace are not approved for IU institutional data, even if the data has been anonymized.

4.6 Accessibility🔗

Students who need accommodations should work with IU’s Student Accessibility Services. Please contact the instructor as early as possible so course work can be coordinated appropriately.
