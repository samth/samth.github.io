5.1 Codex Access🔗

For course work, use Codex through IU’s ChatGPT Edu workspace with your @iu.edu email address. Do not use a personal ChatGPT account or API-key login for course work unless an assignment explicitly says to do so.

IU’s About ChatGPT Edu at IU Knowledge Base article identifies ChatGPT for Education (ChatGPT Edu) as a secure, privacy-focused version of OpenAI’s platform for responsible use with university data, and it explicitly lists Codex among the supported ChatGPT Edu capabilities. IU’s AI at IU page says the listed AI tools are recommended for IU faculty, staff, and students when used with an @iu.edu account, and says information entered in IU’s ChatGPT Edu workspace is not used to train AI models.

The IU KB article says users receive 5,000 credits weekly by default. For this course, fill out IU’s ChatGPT Edu credit request form and request the free increase to 10,000 credits per week.

To use Codex on the web:

  • Go to chatgpt.com/codex.

  • Sign in with your @iu.edu email address.

  • When asked how to log in, choose Indiana University, then complete IU Login and Duo if prompted.

  • If asked to choose a workspace, choose the Indiana University workspace associated with your IU email address.

  • If ChatGPT asks what to do with an existing account that uses your IU email address, read the migration prompt carefully before choosing.

To use the Codex CLI:

  • Run codex login.

  • Choose Sign in with ChatGPT.

  • Complete the browser sign-in flow with your @iu.edu email address, choosing Indiana University for IU SSO if prompted.

  • Run codex login status to confirm that Codex is using ChatGPT sign-in rather than an API key.

For the Codex desktop app or IDE extension, choose Sign in with ChatGPT and complete the browser sign-in flow with your @iu.edu email address. In the ChatGPT desktop app, choose Codex from the ChatGPT product selector when working with codebase context and developer tools.

If you cannot access Codex with your IU email address, contact the course staff instead of buying a separate subscription. For IU ChatGPT Edu access or account migration issues, contact your campus Support Center. For ChatGPT Edu service problems after you are in the IU workspace, contact OpenAI Support at support@openai.com.

OpenAI’s authentication documentation describes ChatGPT sign-in, CLI login, IDE-extension login, and API-key login: https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/auth. OpenAI’s quickstart describes the ChatGPT desktop and web entry points: https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/quickstart.

The public page will be published at https://samth.github.io/agentic-programming/.
