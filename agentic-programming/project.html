6 Final Project🔗

For your final project, you will build a new piece of software that you want to have. The basic idea is that you will pick some piece of software that you’ve used or seen, and that could be better customized to your needs, and then you will use coding agents to help you build it. You will be expected to use the tools and techniques that we have discussed in class, including testing, reviewing, and iterating on your code.

For example, you might want to build a new calendar application, or a workout tracker, or a playlist organizer, or a tool to help you manage your finances.

Each student will complete an individual project.

The result will need to contain 3 parts.

  • The implementation of the tool itself, which should be a working piece of software that you can use.

  • A way to validate the correctness of the tool, which could be a set of tests, or a verification approach, or a way to ensure that it matches the behavior of an existing tool.

  • An interactive web page or a video walkthrough that demonstrates the tool and explains how it works.

The project will be graded based on your ambition for building something new, the comprehensiveness of your validation, and the clarity of your demonstration. You will be expected to submit your code, your validation, and your demonstration by the last day of the semester, before finals week.

Submit a link to your GitHub repository. The repository should contain your code, your validation, and either your demonstration or a link to it.

During the last week of class, we will have an opportunity to demo your projects to the class and to show what you’ve built.
